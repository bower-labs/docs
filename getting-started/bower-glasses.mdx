Bower Glasses let you capture and talk to Bower without putting anything down. They run on Mentra Live smart glasses: a camera, microphones, and speakers in a pair of frames, with no display. The glasses pair with the Bower app on your phone, and everything they capture lands in your workspace.

## What you need

- **Mentra Live glasses.** In the Bower app they appear as **Bower Glasses**.
- **The Bower app** on your phone, signed in to your workspace.
- **A Wi-Fi network** the glasses can join. Photos and video travel over Wi-Fi, not Bluetooth.

## Set up your glasses

Turn the glasses on and put them in pairing mode, then open the **Devices** tab in the Bower app. Tap your glasses when they appear in the list. The first time you connect a pair, Bower walks you through setup:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bluetooth">
    Pair the glasses with your phone. On Android, allow Bluetooth and Location when asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wi-Fi">
    Tap **Scan for networks**, choose your network, and tap **Join**. The glasses need a network to capture, so you can't skip this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Voice">
    Tap **Enable voice commands** to download the speech model, about 75 MB, once. Voice commands are recognized on your phone. You can skip this step and turn voice commands on later in **Settings → Communication**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Firmware">
    Bower checks for a glasses update and installs it. Keep the screen open until it finishes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name">
    Give the glasses a nickname if you like, then tap **Start Capturing**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On iPhone, Bower's voice plays through the glasses only if Mentra Live is also connected in **Settings → Bluetooth**. Otherwise you'll hear it from the phone speaker.

## Capture with the button

| Press | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Press** | Takes a photo. |
| **Double-press** | Starts recording video. Press again to stop. |
| **Press and hold** | Starts a live session with Bower. Hold again to end it. |

Captures save to the workspace selected on the **Dashboard** tab. If no workspace is selected, the app asks you to choose one first. A video saved through your phone can be up to 2 minutes long.

## Capture with your voice

With voice commands on and the Bower app open, say a command:

- *"Take a photo"*
- *"Start recording"* and *"Stop recording"*
- *"Start voice memo"* and *"Stop microphone"*
- *"Go live"*

You can also say *"Hey Bower"* and then ask a question. Bower answers out loud while the conversation runs in the background, and **Open the Live screen** takes you to the full live view. The conversation ends after 3 minutes without activity.

<Note>
Voice commands only listen while the Bower app is open on your phone. During a live session, Bower's live agent handles everything you say instead.
</Note>

## Go live with Bower

Press and hold the button, say *"Go live"*, or tap **Execute Protocol** on a protocol in the app. Bower sees through the glasses camera, hears you, and answers through the speakers. You can ask questions, [run a protocol](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode#execution-mode-running-a-protocol) step by step, take snapshots, and record clips. See [Live agent mode](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode) for everything you can do.

While a clip is recording, the **REC** indicator shows on the live screen in the Bower app, not in the glasses.

If the glasses aren't on Wi-Fi yet, the session starts voice only and adds video once they connect.

Bower Glasses have no display, so anything Bower shows you, such as *"show me the video for this protocol"*, opens on the live screen in the Bower app.

## When there's no Wi-Fi

If there's no network nearby, turn on **Glasses hotspot** on the **Devices** tab so your phone connects to the glasses directly. Captures wait on your phone until you're back online, and live sessions can hear you but can't see.

Anything you capture while your phone is offline is queued and uploads once you reconnect.

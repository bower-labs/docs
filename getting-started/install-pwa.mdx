We recommend the [Bower app](/getting-started/mobile) on your phone. If you'd rather not install an app, open [app.bowerlabs.ai](https://app.bowerlabs.ai) in your phone's browser. You can capture, chat with Bower, go live, and browse your workspace there too.

The browser version can't pair with [Bower Glasses](/bower-glasses/overview). For that, use the app.

## Signing in

Sign in with the same account you use on your computer. If you're already signed in on your computer, open the onboarding checklist, expand a phone step, and choose **Use Bower in your phone's browser instead** under the QR code. Scan the new code with your phone's camera and a single-use link signs you in, with no password to retype.

Bower suggests the app while you use the browser. In Safari on iPhone, a banner at the top of the page offers it. In other phone browsers, and when you open Bower from your home screen, the **Capture** tab shows a **Get the Bower app** card. Close either one to keep using the browser.

## Finding your way around

The browser version has a bottom navigation bar with four tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Capture** | Record voice, take a photo, shoot a video, or type a note. |
| **Chat** | Chat with Bower, typed or by voice. The waveform button beside the send (arrow) button starts a [live session](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode). |
| **Workspace** | Browse, search, and open notes, collections, protocols, and attachments. |
| **Settings** | Your account, profile, and preferences. |

When you start a voice or video recording, the navigation bar and header step out of the way for a full-screen capture view, then reappear when you're done.

## Add it to your home screen

Adding Bower to your home screen gives it its own icon. On iPhone and iPad, it then opens full-screen, without Safari's address bar.

### iPhone and iPad (Safari)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Bower in Safari">
    Go to [app.bowerlabs.ai](https://app.bowerlabs.ai) in Safari.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the share button">
    Tap the share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add to Home Screen">
    Scroll down in the share menu and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Android (Chrome)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Bower in Chrome">
    Go to [app.bowerlabs.ai](https://app.bowerlabs.ai) in Chrome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the menu">
    Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add to Home screen">
    Tap **Add to Home screen** and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
On Android, Chrome may offer to install the Bower app from Google Play instead. That's the [Bower app](/getting-started/mobile), not a home-screen shortcut.
</Note>

Everything you capture syncs to your workspace once it uploads.

The Bower app is how you use Bower at the bench. It works on the same account and the same workspaces as your computer, and everything you capture syncs to your workspace once it uploads.

<AppStoreLinks />

## Sign in

Sign in with the account you use on your computer: **Continue with Apple** (iPhone only), **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Microsoft**, or your email and password. If you choose Google, Microsoft, or Apple and your email already has a Bower account, the app asks you to sign in the usual way once, then connects that provider to your account.

New to Bower? Tap **Sign up**.

## Choose a workspace

Pick a workspace at the top of the **Dashboard** tab. Everything you capture, including from [Bower Glasses](/bower-glasses/overview), saves to this workspace. You can also switch from the title of the **Workspace** tab.

## Finding your way around

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Choose your workspace, start a protocol, and see your glasses' status. |
| **Devices** | Pair and manage [Bower Glasses](/bower-glasses/overview). |
| **Workspace** | Browse and search notes, protocols, equipment, files (under **Artifacts**), and live sessions. |
| **Profile** | Your account and **Settings**. |

The chat bubble at the top right of the **Dashboard** and **Workspace** tabs opens Bower chat.

Tap **+** in the middle of the tab bar to open the capture menu, then turn the dial to **Vision**, **Voice**, **Live**, **Text**, or **Upload**. See [Mobile capture](/capture/mobile-capture) and [Live agent mode](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode).

## Captures are safe offline

When you save a capture, the app keeps it on your phone and uploads it in the background. If you lose signal, it waits and uploads once you're back online. A banner at the top of the **Workspace** tab shows anything still waiting.

## Prefer not to install an app?

You can [use Bower in your phone's browser](/getting-started/install-pwa) instead. It can't pair with Bower Glasses.

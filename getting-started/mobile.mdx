Your phone is where you capture at the bench and where you go live with Bower. You can use the Bower app or open Bower in your phone's browser. Both use the same account and the same workspace as your computer, and everything syncs in real time.

## The Bower app

The Bower app is what we recommend. It's also the app you use to pair [Bower Glasses](/getting-started/bower-glasses).

<AppStoreLinks />

Sign in with the same account you use on your computer. The app has four tabs and a capture button:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Choose your workspace and start a protocol. |
| **Devices** | Pair and manage Bower Glasses. |
| **Workspace** | Browse, search, and open notes, collections, protocols, and attachments. |
| **Profile** | Your account and **Settings**. |

Tap **+** in the middle of the tab bar to open the capture menu, then turn the dial to **Vision**, **Voice**, **Live**, **Text**, or **Upload**. **Live** starts a [live agent session](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode).

If you lose signal, anything you capture waits on your phone and uploads once you're back online.

## In your phone's browser

If you'd rather not install an app, open [app.bowerlabs.ai](https://app.bowerlabs.ai) in your phone's browser. Bower gives you a bottom navigation bar with four tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Capture** | Record voice, take a photo, shoot a video, or type a note. See [Mobile capture](/capture/mobile-capture). |
| **Bower** | Chat with Bower, typed or by voice, and start a live session with the waveform button beside **Send**. |
| **Workspace** | Browse, search, and open everything: notes, collections, protocols, and attachments. Search runs full-text and semantically as you type. |
| **Settings** | Your account, profile, and preferences. |

When you start a voice or video recording, the navigation bar and header step out of the way for a full-screen capture view, then reappear when you're done.

You can [add Bower to your home screen](/getting-started/install-pwa) so it opens full-screen with its own icon.

## Signing in on your phone from your computer

If you're already signed in on your computer, the onboarding checklist shows a **QR code**. Scan it with your phone's camera and a secure, single-use link signs you in to Bower in your phone's browser. No password retyping, and it works for enterprise SSO accounts.

title: "Collections"
description: "Group related notes, attachments, and protocols by sample batch, experiment, or whatever structure matches how you work."
---

A collection is a named group of notes, attachments, protocols, and other collections. Use them the way you'd use folders on your drive: by sample batch, experiment, protocol run, or date range.

Each item lives in one collection, or at the top level of the workspace. Collections can be nested, for example a project at the top level with a collection per sample batch inside it.

## Creating a collection

In the workspace view, click **Add** and choose **New collection**, or right-click empty space. To create one inside another, right-click the parent collection and choose **New collection inside**.

## Working with collections

Moving items in and out, reordering, renaming, and deleting collections all work the same way as for any other item. See [Organizing your workspace](/organisation/organising-workspace).

To find something inside a collection, open it and use the **Scope**, **Type**, and other filters. **Cmd+K** / **Ctrl+K** searches the whole workspace.

## Visibility

By default, everyone in the workspace can see a collection. You can restrict any collection to specific people, or make it private to yourself, from the **Share** dialog. See [Sharing notes and collections](/organisation/sharing-artifacts) for the Share dialog, and [Workspace permissions overview](/workspaces-and-account/permissions-overview) for how per-item access fits with workspace roles.

If you need strict data isolation between projects or teams, use a separate [workspace](/workspaces-and-account/create-workspace).

Click **Workspace** in the left rail to open the workspace view. It shows the contents of one collection at a time, or the top level of your workspace. This page covers everything you can do there, with the mouse and the keyboard. Shortcuts use **Cmd** on Mac and **Ctrl** on Windows/Linux.

## Finding your way around

- **Breadcrumb.** The top bar shows where you are. Click any level to go back up. When you open an item from the workspace, its page shows the same breadcrumb and a back arrow to its collection. When you open an item from the **Protocols** list or from **Protocol runs**, the trail starts there instead, and the back arrow returns you to that list. See [Finding your way back](/organisation/protocols#finding-your-way-back).
- **Search.** The search bar in the top bar opens the [command palette](/ai-and-search/command-palette).
- **Add.** The **Add** button creates a collection or captures something into the collection you have open. You can also right-click empty space for the same menu, or drop files anywhere on the page to upload them there.

### Views

Choose a view with the switch on the right of the header:

| View | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Folder view** | Icons and names, for scanning many items |
| **Card view** | A preview of each item: the start of a note, an image thumbnail, the extracted text of a PDF or code file, or a video poster |
| **List view** | A table with columns such as **Location**, **Created by**, and dates. Drag a column header's handle to reorder columns, or focus the handle and press **Left** / **Right** |

In Folder view and Card view, collections sit in their own section at the top under every sort, under **Collections**, with your files below them under **Files**. Each collection shows how many items it holds and when it last changed. The header counts both, for example "7 collections · 8 files".

Your view, sort, and filters are one choice for the whole workspace. Bower remembers them for you and keeps them as you move between collections. On a phone, you get Folder view and List view.

### Scope, filters, and sort

- **Scope** chooses between the items directly in this collection and everything inside it, including sub-collections. At the top level, the options are **Top level** and **Everything**.
- **Type** filters by kind: notes, protocols, collections, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, images, video, audio, code, archives, equipment, links, and **Instrument data** (microscopy, flow cytometry, mass spectrometry, sequencing, structures, and more). Documents cover PDF, Word, and TXT files; spreadsheets cover Excel, CSV, and TSV; presentations cover PowerPoint and Keynote; code covers R, Python, MATLAB, and notebooks; archives cover ZIP, RAR, 7z, and TAR. [Links](/organisation/workspace-items#links) are shortcuts to pages outside Bower and open in a new tab.
- **Modified**, **Created by**, and **Tags** filter by date, the member who created the item, and tag. (The **Created by** filter used to be labeled **People**.) On a narrow window, the filters fold into one filter button that shows how many are active.
- **Sort** orders by **Name**, **Last modified**, **Date created**, or **Type**, in either direction. In Folder view and Card view, collections stay in their own section above files under every sort. In List view, sorting by **Name** or **Type** lists collections before files, and sorting by **Last modified** or **Date created** mixes them into one date order, so newest first puts the newest collection or file at the top. The list on a phone follows the List view rule. A collection's **Last modified** date changes when you rename or edit the collection itself, not when you add items inside it.

## Selecting items

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Select one item | Click it |
| Add or remove one item | **Cmd+click** |
| Select a range | **Shift+click**, or **Shift+Arrow** |
| Select a group | Drag a rectangle across empty space (Folder and Card views) |
| Select every loaded item | **Cmd+A** |
| Move between items | **Arrow keys** |
| Clear the selection | **Escape**, or click **Clear selection** in the action bar |

In List view, a single click opens a row. Use the row's checkbox to select it instead.

## Acting on items

Open an item with a double-click, or with **Enter**. Everything else is in two places:

- **The action bar** appears when you select something. It shows the actions that apply to your selection. A grayed-out action tells you why it can't run, for example "Merge works on notes only."
- **The item menu** opens with a right-click, **Shift+F10**, the context menu key, or a long-press on a touchscreen. It's the same menu on tiles, cards, rows, and the page tree.

| Action | What it does | Shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** / **Open in new tab** | Open the item | **Enter** |
| **Rename** | Edit the name in place. **Enter** saves; **Escape** or clicking away cancels | **F2** |
| **Move to...** | Move the selection to another collection | |
| **Copy to...** | Duplicate the selection into another collection. The originals stay where they are | |
| **Copy link** | Copy a direct link to each item | |
| **Add to** *collection* | Create something inside this collection, including another collection *(collections only)* | |
| **Merge** | Combine 2 to 20 selected notes into one | |
| **Add to favorites** | Add the item to **Favorites** in the page tree | |
| **Download** | Download the original files *(uploaded files only; open a note to [export](/organisation/export) it)* | |
| **Delete** | Move the selection to Trash, after you confirm | **Delete** or **Backspace** |

### Copy, cut, and paste

**Cmd+C** copies the selected items and **Cmd+X** cuts them. Open the collection you want, then press **Cmd+V**, or right-click empty space and choose **Paste**.

- A copy makes duplicates. You can paste the same copy more than once.
- A cut moves the items. Cut items look faded until you paste them. Press **Escape** to cancel a cut.

This uses Bower's own clipboard, so it carries items between collections, not between apps. Copying text you've selected on the page still works as usual.

### Drag and drop

Drag items onto a collection, in the workspace or in the page tree, to move them there. Bower stops a move that can't work, such as a collection into itself or into a collection you can only view, before anything changes.

If a collection with the same name is already there, Bower renames the collection you moved.

### Undo

After a move, paste, or delete, a message at the bottom of the screen offers **Undo** for a few seconds. Undoing a move also restores a collection name Bower changed.

## The page tree

The panel on the left is the page tree. It opens with the workspace view and closes when you leave it; on a narrow window, open it from the rail.

- **Favorites** lists the items you've added to favorites, once you have some. Favorites are personal: your teammates don't see them, and they follow you across devices. You can keep up to 50, and drag them to reorder.
- **Shared with me** lists items shared with you that sit in a collection you can't open, so they have no place in the tree. It appears only when there are some.
- **The tree** shows every collection and its contents. Drag an item to reorder it, or onto a collection to nest it. To reorder from the keyboard, focus an item and press **Alt+Up** / **Alt+Down**. The order is shared: everyone in the workspace sees the same arrangement.
- **Trash** is at the bottom of the panel.

## Trash

Deleting moves items to Trash. They stay there for **30 days**, then Bower deletes them permanently.

- **Deleting a collection** moves everything inside it to Trash too.
- **The Trash page** lists everything in Trash, with search, sort, and a type filter. Restore items from there, or delete them permanently before the 30 days are up.
- **Restoring an item whose collection is also in Trash** puts it at the top level of the workspace.

If you need to keep data longer, [export it](/organisation/export) before the 30 days are up.

Spreadsheets in Bower are full workbooks you can edit right here: formulas,
formatting, multiple sheets, charts and pivot tables. Your team can work in
the same spreadsheet at the same time, and Bower can read the data, answer
questions about it and keep it up to date.

## Get a spreadsheet into Bower

There are four ways to start.

- **Start from scratch.** Choose **Create (+) > Spreadsheet > Start from
  scratch** for an empty sheet.
- **Import a file.** Choose **Create (+) > Spreadsheet > Import a file** and
  pick a `.csv` or `.xlsx` file. Every sheet in the file comes across.
- **Upload it anywhere.** A `.csv` or `.xlsx` you upload with **Import files**
  on a collection or the Artifacts page, or attach in Bower Chat, becomes a
  spreadsheet automatically.
- **Convert a file you already uploaded.** Open the file and choose **Convert
  to spreadsheet** from its menu. Bower creates a new spreadsheet next to it,
  with the same sharing settings, and leaves the original file as it was.
  Files uploaded before spreadsheets were available are not converted for you.

Spreadsheets live at the top of your workspace or inside a collection.

### While it sets up

Turning a file into a spreadsheet takes a few seconds, longer for large
files. You never have to wait: keep working, and the spreadsheet's icon shows
a small spinner wherever it appears (your collection, the page tree, recent
items and chat) until it is ready. Bower lets you know when it is done.

If you open it early, you see **Setting up your spreadsheet**, and the page
switches to the spreadsheet by itself when it is ready. Your original file is
always kept with the spreadsheet.

### Files that stay as files

Some files are kept as regular attachments, and Bower tells you why when you
upload them.

| File | Why it stays a file |
| --- | --- |
| Macro-enabled workbooks (`.xlsm`) | Bower cannot keep macros working. Save a copy as `.xlsx` to edit it in Bower. |
| CSV over 16 MB, Excel over 25 MB | Too large to edit as a spreadsheet. |
| Files attached to a note, equipment or a protocol | Spreadsheets live in a workspace or a collection. |

You can still download these files and ask Bower about them.

### If a file cannot be set up

Occasionally a file cannot become a spreadsheet, for example a CSV with more
than 200,000 cells, or a damaged file. The spreadsheet shows an error icon, and
opening it tells you why. From there you can **Download original** to get
back exactly what you uploaded, or **Retry import** if the problem was
temporary.

### What comes across from Excel

Cell values and formulas come across intact, including named ranges and
tables. A few things do not survive the import:

- **Pivot tables** are not kept. You can build new ones in Bower.
- **Charts** may not all come across. Check them after importing.
- **Links to external data** (connections and linked workbooks) are dropped,
  so the spreadsheet no longer refreshes from its source.
- Printer settings are not kept.

## Work in a spreadsheet

The spreadsheet page works like the spreadsheet tools you already know.

- **Edit** cells, write formulas, format numbers and text, and use multiple
  sheets (tabs along the bottom).
- **Organize your data** with sort, filter, data validation, conditional
  formatting, tables, notes and hyperlinks. Add images, charts and pivot tables.
- **Find** anything with the search box above the grid. Press Enter for the
  next match and Shift+Enter for the previous one.
- **Discover features** with the features search, and see every shortcut
  with the keyboard shortcuts button.
- **Rename** the spreadsheet by editing its title.

### Saving and working together

There is no save button: every change saves as you go, and the indicator in
the header shows when it is saving and when it is saved. You need to be
online to edit.

Everyone with edit access can work in the same spreadsheet at the same time,
and you see where your teammates are in the sheet as they work.

### Version history

Choose **Version history** from the spreadsheet's menu to see who changed
what and when, and to restore an earlier version.

### Share, export and print

- **Manage access** to decide who can view and who can edit. People with view
  access see the spreadsheet but cannot change it.
- **Export** as an Excel file (`.xlsx`), or export the current sheet as a CSV.
  Exporting needs edit access.
- **Print** from the spreadsheet's menu.
- **Move, copy or delete** the spreadsheet from the same menu, like anything
  else in Bower.

## Ask Bower about your spreadsheets

Bower understands your spreadsheets the way you do: by sheet, column header
and row.

### What you can ask

- **Questions about the data.** "What's the average yield in the Batch B
  sheet?" "Which samples are below 0.5?" Bower reads the live values,
  including what your formulas calculate, and tells you which sheet and range
  its answer came from.
- **About what you have selected.** When a spreadsheet is open, the chat box
  shows it along with the cells you have selected, and updates as your
  selection changes. Ask "summarize these" and Bower works on exactly those
  cells. You can remove the selection and keep the spreadsheet in the
  conversation.
- **About charts.** Bower can read the charts on a sheet and explain what they
  show.
- **Deeper analysis.** For statistics such as fits and models, or figures for
  a note or report, Bower runs the analysis on the values you point it to.
  For a large range, it asks you to narrow it down first.
- **About a file you just uploaded.** Attach a spreadsheet in chat and ask
  about it in the same message. If it is still setting up, Bower answers from
  your original file and tells you so.

Bower also flags cells with errors, such as `#DIV/0!`, when they affect an
answer.

### What Bower can change

Bower can **add a row or update one**. It finds the row by the value in the
first column (for example a sample ID) and fills in the columns you name.
Before it changes anything:

- it asks you which row you mean if the value matches more than one row, or
  none;
- it checks with you before overwriting a cell that already has a value;
- it confirms the change only after checking it landed.

The change appears straight away for everyone who has the spreadsheet open.
Every change Bower makes is recorded with the before and after values.

Other changes, such as formatting, formulas, charts, sorting and filtering,
are not available yet. Bower tells you when you ask for one. Guests can ask
Bower about a spreadsheet but cannot have it make changes.

### Undo a change Bower made

When Bower changes a spreadsheet from chat, a **Spreadsheet updated** card
appears in the conversation. It lists each change Bower made.

Press **Undo** on the card to reverse the whole batch of changes. The
spreadsheet goes back exactly as it was before Bower's change. While this
happens, the spreadsheet beside the chat shows **Undoing Bower's change...**
and then reloads with the earlier version. The card then says **Undone**.

The undo appears in the spreadsheet's [version history](#version-history) as a **Reverted from**
entry. The version with Bower's change is still there, so you can restore it
if you change your mind.

#### When undo is not possible

Undo only works while nobody has edited the spreadsheet since Bower's change.
That includes you, a teammate and another change by Bower. If the spreadsheet
has changed, nothing is undone, and the card says so. Choose **Open version
history** to find the version you want and restore it.

A card sometimes lists the changes with no Undo button. That happens when
someone else edited the spreadsheet while Bower was making its change, or when
the change could not be undone safely. Use version history instead.

#### Good to know about undo

- Undo is available for 24 hours after Bower's change.
- Only the person Bower made the change for can undo it, and only if they can
  edit the spreadsheet.
- If Bower cannot confirm whether the undo happened, for example because the
  connection dropped, the card tells you and offers version history. Check the
  spreadsheet before you try again.
- If the card says nothing was changed, Undo stays on the card so you can try
  again.
- Teammates who have the spreadsheet open see a notice and need to refresh the
  page to see the earlier version.
- Undo is the button on the card. Bower cannot undo a change if you ask it to
  in chat, and undoing by voice in Live mode is not available yet.

### Live mode

In Live mode, Bower knows which spreadsheet, sheet and cells you have on
screen, so you can ask about them by voice. Answers are short and spoken.
Before any change, Bower reads back what it is about to do and waits for your
yes.

## Good to know

- Spreadsheets need an internet connection to edit.
- Text in your cells is treated as data. Bower never follows instructions
  written inside a spreadsheet.

Bower uses a small number of cookies and browser storage items to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the product is used.

## Cookie consent

What you see in the Bower app depends on where you are, based on your IP address. **This starts on 16 November 2026.** Until then, everyone sees the banner and browser analytics stay off until you choose.

**In the EU, the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland** (including Guernsey, Jersey, the Isle of Man, Gibraltar, and EU territories with their own country codes), and wherever your location is unknown, you'll see a consent banner and browser analytics stay off until you choose:

- **Accept all**: enables analytics cookies alongside essential ones.
- **Essential only**: only the cookies Bower needs to function. No analytics cookies, session replay, or click tracking in your browser. This does not switch off [server-side product events](#not-a-cookie-server-side-product-events).

**Everywhere else,** browser analytics are on by default. The banner may flash briefly while Bower checks your location. You can switch them off at any time in **Settings > Security**.

Your explicit choice always wins and is never overridden. If you're on the default and later open Bower from one of the regions above, browser analytics switch off and the banner returns. This applies to the app only; on bowerlabs.ai, analytics wait for consent everywhere.

## Cookie categories

### Essential (always active)

These cookies are required for Bower to work. They cannot be disabled.

| Cookie / Storage | Purpose | Duration |
|-----------------|---------|----------|
| Firebase Auth token | Keeps you signed in and verifies your identity on every request | Session (refreshed automatically) |
| `workspaceId` (localStorage) | Remembers which workspace you last used | Persistent |
| `bower_cookie_consent` (localStorage) | Stores your analytics preference, and whether it was your choice or the regional default | Persistent |
| `chakra-ui-color-mode` (localStorage) | Remembers your light/dark theme choice | Persistent |
| `ui-store` (localStorage) | Sidebar state and UI preferences | Persistent |

### Analytics

These cookies help us understand how Bower is used so we can improve it. In the EU, the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, they are **only activated after you click "Accept all"**. Elsewhere they are **on by default** (see [Cookie consent](#cookie-consent)).

| Cookie / Storage | Purpose | Provider | Duration |
|-----------------|---------|----------|----------|
| PostHog cookies | Product analytics, session recording, heatmaps | [PostHog](https://posthog.com/privacy) | Session / 1 year |
| Google Analytics cookies | Marketing-site analytics and signup-funnel attribution. On the app these fire only on pre-sign-in pages, follow the same regional rule as PostHog, and stop permanently once you sign in. | [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) | Up to 2 years |

**What we track:** page views, feature usage (which tools you use and how often), UI interactions, and error rates. We use this to prioritize improvements and fix bugs.

**What analytics events contain:** action names such as "Note Created", plus internal IDs and counts. They never include the content of your notes, files, or chats, or their titles and names. This applies in every workspace.

- **Click tracking** records which element you clicked, not its text.
- **Error reports** keep the error type and code location, not the message.
- **Session replay**, when it is on, records page layout and clicks. All text, form inputs, images, and video are masked. Console logs and network contents are not recorded. One limit: accessibility labels on buttons and links can still appear, and these sometimes name an item, such as a file name on a **Remove** button.
- **Voice recordings and file contents** are never sent.

**In [Restricted mode](/trust-center/hipaa-compliance) workspaces,** session replay, heatmaps, and click tracking are off entirely, enforced in the browser.

### Not a cookie: server-side product events

When you use Bower, our servers record events about your own actions in the product, such as signing up, sending a chat message, or a file finishing processing, and send them to PostHog. We use them to run and improve Bower, to keep our usage figures complete, and to find failures. **This is server-side.** Apart from the sign-up details described below, it sets no cookie and reads no browser or device storage. It does not depend on the consent banner or the **Analytics cookies** setting, in any region.

**What these events contain:** the action name, internal user and workspace IDs, counts, durations, and whether the action succeeded. Like every analytics event, they never include the content of your notes, files, or chats, or the titles and file names of those items. This applies in every workspace, including [Restricted mode](/trust-center/hipaa-compliance) workspaces, where these events continue.

Four things go beyond IDs:

- **Request details.** Events from a request your device makes carry its IP address and browser or device type. PostHog keeps the IP address and uses it to derive your approximate location (country, region, and city).
- **Email addresses, on a few events.** Your sign-up and profile updates, billing and trial notices for the workspace owner, and invitations to a paid seat (the inviter's and the invitee's address) include an email address so our team can follow up. When the server checks whether a feature is turned on for you, it sends your email address for that check only, and PostHog does not store it.
- **Your name and ORCID iD.** Your sign-up and profile updates include your display name, and your ORCID iD if you add one.
- **Details from your browser, at sign-up.** On your first page load, whatever your cookie choice, the app saves a first-visit record in your browser's local storage: a random visitor ID, the referring site, the landing page, and campaign and ad click IDs. The sign-up event includes that record and details the sign-up page reads from your browser: screen and window size, color depth, language, time zone, processor core count, graphics hardware, and how the form was filled in (time on the form, mouse movement, whether anything was pasted, and a keystroke count, never the keys). We use these to attribute sign-ups and to detect abuse.

A few events about failed sign-in attempts are recorded before you are signed in. Opening a shared link records an event with the viewer's IP address and browser or device type, even if the viewer has no Bower account.

**Lawful basis:** our legitimate interest in running, securing, and improving Bower, and performance of our contract with you for account and billing events. You can object by writing to [privacy@bowerlabs.ai](mailto:privacy@bowerlabs.ai). The [Privacy Policy](https://bowerlabs.ai/privacy) governs.

### Not a cookie: AI quality monitoring

Bower retains AI request and response text in a third-party observability service for quality monitoring. **This is server-side.** It sets no cookie and no browser storage, so it is not covered by the consent banner above and cannot be changed from this page. It is disclosed in full on [AI & your data](/trust-center/ai-data-handling#ai-quality-monitoring-and-what-it-retains), and the provider is named on the [sub-processor list](/trust-center/sub-processors).

## Managing your cookies

- **On first visit:** use the consent banner if you see one. Otherwise analytics are on by default.
- **After sign-in:** go to **Settings > Security** to change your analytics preference. Switching off stops further collection in your browser but does not delete Google Analytics cookies already set. [Server-side product events](#not-a-cookie-server-side-product-events) continue.
- **In your browser:** clear Bower's cookies and localStorage at any time. This signs you out and resets your consent preference.

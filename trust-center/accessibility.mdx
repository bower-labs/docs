Bower is used by researchers, clinicians, and students who work in very different ways. We want the product to be usable regardless of how you navigate it: keyboard only, screen reader, magnification, voice, or reduced motion.

## Conformance target

Bower targets **[Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)**.

<Note>
  **We have not yet completed a formal evaluation against that target, so we make no conformance claim today.** This page states what we are building toward and how to reach us, not a result. When we have completed an assessment we will publish the outcome here, including where we fall short.
</Note>

WCAG has no certifying body. Conformance is always declared by the product owner, which is exactly why a claim is only worth what the evaluation behind it is worth. We would rather tell you we have not finished than publish a badge with nothing underneath it.

## What we are building toward

These are our design targets. They are not audited results.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Keyboard navigation" icon="keyboard">
    Core flows, including capture, the editor, and search, operable without a pointer, with a visible focus indicator throughout. The [keyboard shortcuts reference](/capture/keyboard-shortcuts) covers what exists today.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Colour contrast" icon="circle-half-stroke">
    The Level AA ratios of 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and interface components, in both light and dark themes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Semantic structure" icon="list-tree">
    Headings, landmarks, lists, and tables built on native HTML semantics, so a screen reader can navigate by structure rather than reading linearly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Text alternatives" icon="image">
    Alt text on images and attachments. Bower's AI-generated descriptions can give you a starting point for a figure or photo, which you then correct.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Resize and reflow" icon="magnifying-glass-plus">
    Usable at 200% zoom without loss of content or function.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reduced motion" icon="wind">
    Animation that respects the reduced-motion preference set in your operating system.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Known gaps

Two parts of Bower are visual by nature, and we would rather name them now than have you discover them.

| Area | Gap | Where it stands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annotations and diagrams | Drawing on an image is a pointer-driven canvas. There is no keyboard-only equivalent. | No workaround today. On our list. |
| Knowledge graph | The graph is rendered visually. The same relationships appear as text in each note's linked-items list, which is the accessible equivalent. | Alternative available |
| Imported documents | PDFs, Word files, and images you bring in carry their own accessibility characteristics. Bower cannot make an inaccessible source document accessible. | Outside our control |

## Tell us about a barrier

If you hit something you cannot use, we want to hear about it. A specific report is worth more to us than a general audit.

- **Email:** [accessibility@bowerlabs.ai](mailto:accessibility@bowerlabs.ai)
- **What helps:** the page or feature, what you were trying to do, and your browser and assistive technology if you use one.
- **What we do:** we acknowledge accessibility reports within two business days and tell you our plan and timeline, or give you a workaround.

If you need a **VPAT or Accessibility Conformance Report** for a procurement or institutional review, email the same address. We will tell you honestly where our evaluation stands rather than send you a document we cannot stand behind.

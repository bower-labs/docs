## Why this page is a pointer

Until August 2026 the Terms existed in two hand-maintained copies, here and on the website. They drifted: the two versions named different governing jurisdictions, which is a defect in the dispute-resolution clause rather than a formatting difference. Now the website holds the only copy, and this page links to it.

## Why this page is a pointer

Until August 2026 the Privacy Policy existed in two hand-maintained copies, here and on the website. They drifted, including on what they promised about AI training: one version said your content is not used to train "public or third-party" AI models, the other said "AI models" without qualification. There is now one copy, and this page points at it rather than restating it.

The same applies to the [Terms of Service](/trust-center/terms-of-service).

## What is still explained here

- [AI and your data](/trust-center/ai-data-handling): which providers process what, and what is retained
- [Data privacy](/trust-center/data-privacy): encryption, workspace isolation, access control, deletion
- [Sub-processors](/trust-center/sub-processors): every third party that processes data on our behalf
- [Cookies](/trust-center/cookies): what is set, and where it requires consent
- [Restricted mode](/trust-center/hipaa-compliance): the controls that apply to PHI workspaces

Where one of those pages and the Privacy Policy describe the same thing, the Privacy Policy governs.

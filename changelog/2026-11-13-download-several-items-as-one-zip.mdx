**2026-11-13 · Workspace**

You can now select several items or collections in your workspace and download them as one ZIP file. It's on for every workspace.

## What's new

- **Every kind of item.** Notes and protocols come as PDFs, with a protocol's notes beside it. Spreadsheets come as Excel files, equipment as a PDF of its details, uploaded files exactly as you uploaded them, and links in one `links.csv` file.
- **Collections become folders.** The ZIP file keeps the shape of your workspace, like a download from a cloud drive.
- **Three ways to start.** Click **Download** in the action bar, right-click a selection, or choose **Download** from a collection's **⋮** menu.
- **Names you recognize.** One collection or one item on its own downloads under its own name.
- **Spreadsheets for anyone who can view them.** If you can open a spreadsheet, it comes with your download.
- **Keep working.** Bower prepares the file in the background and saves it to your computer when it's ready.

See [Downloading several items](/organisation/organising-workspace#downloading-several-items) for what the ZIP file holds and how large a download can be.

**Shipped 2026-10-05 · Organization**

The page that maps how your notes, protocols, attachments, equipment,
collections and people connect is now called the **Workspace visualizer**. It
shows the same thing it always has. What changed is the name and where you
open it.

## What's new

- **Open it from your avatar menu.** Click your avatar at the bottom of the
  left rail and choose **Workspace visualizer**, just above **Settings**. The
  icon at the end of the Workspace panel's filter chips has been removed.
- **Show in visualizer.** The action that read **Show in graph** in note,
  protocol, spreadsheet, attachment and collection menus now reads **Show in
  visualizer**. It still opens the visualizer centered on that item. The link
  at the foot of an item's linked-items list reads **Show in visualizer** too,
  and now also opens centered on that item rather than on the whole workspace.
- **Command palette.** The **Go to** entry is now **Workspace visualizer**.
  Typing `graph`, `knowledge graph`, `map` or `connections` still finds it.
- **Pricing.** The plan comparison row now names the Workspace visualizer.
  It's included on every plan, as before.

## Notes

- Nothing about the graph itself has changed: the same nodes, filters, 2D and
  3D views, list view, and permission filtering.
- Bookmarks and links to the page keep working.

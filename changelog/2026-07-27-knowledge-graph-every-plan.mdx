**Shipped 2026-07-27 · Organising your workspace**

The **Knowledge Graph** is no longer an Individual and Team feature. On every
plan (**Starter**, **Individual**, and **Team**) you can open it from the
sidebar and see your workspace as a graph — every note, protocol, attachment,
collection and piece of equipment as a node, and the relationships between them
as edges.

## What changed

The graph page is available on Starter, with the same tools the paid plans get:

- **2D and 3D views**, plus a list view when you'd rather read than explore.
- **Filter** by node type, by relationship type, and by whether a relationship
  is one you made or one Bird inferred — to cut a large workspace down to the
  part you're thinking about.
- **Search** for a specific entity and jump straight to it.
- **Click any node** to open its detail panel, then open the entity itself.
- Open the graph focused on one entity from that entity's own page, to see
  what it connects to.

You only ever see entities you already have access to. The graph respects
per-note privacy and your workspace role — it doesn't widen what you can read.

## Why

The graph is the clearest demonstration of why capturing into one place
compounds: it makes the shape of your work visible. Putting that behind a
paywall meant the people most likely to wonder whether Bower is worth it were
the only ones who couldn't see it. It also renders relationships your workspace
already has, so showing it to you costs us nothing.

## What hasn't changed

- **Inferred AI context graph** remains an Individual and Team feature. On
  Starter the graph draws the relationships **you** created — links you made,
  and protocol, collection and equipment membership. On Individual and Team,
  Bird also infers relationships you never drew by hand, and those edges appear
  in the graph alongside the rest.
- Everything you had already linked stays exactly as it was.

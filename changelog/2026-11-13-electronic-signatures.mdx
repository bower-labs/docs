**2026-11-13 · Trust & compliance**

You can now sign a finished protocol run, or a whole collection, with your Bower password. A signed record is locked, so it can't change until someone reopens it, and every signature, request and unlock is kept in its audit trail.

## What's new

- **Sign a run or a collection.** The person who performed the run, or created the collection, signs first. The dialog shows exactly what you're signing before you enter your password.
- **Ask for review or a witness.** Once the performer has signed, ask a colleague with **Can review** to sign as reviewer or witness. They can sign or decline with a reason, and requests waiting for you appear under **Waiting for your signature** in the sidebar.
- **One password.** You sign with your Bower password, the one you can sign in with. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, Bower asks you to sign in with them again before it saves a new Bower password, in the signing dialog or in **Settings > Security**.
- **Owners and Admins choose who signs.** Turn signing on in **Settings > Electronic signatures**, give members **Can sign** or **Can review**, and confirm each person's identity before granting it.
- **A locked, checkable record.** A signature covers the run's content and the exact bytes of every photo, video and file in it. If anything changes after signing, Bower shows what.
- **Withdraw, reject or unlock with a reason.** Each asks for your Bower password and a reason, and earlier signatures stay in the audit trail.
- **Export the signed record.** Download a ZIP file with a printable PDF, every file as uploaded, a manifest of SHA-256 fingerprints and instructions for checking it yourself.

See [Electronic signatures](/organisation/electronic-signatures) for how to set it up and sign.

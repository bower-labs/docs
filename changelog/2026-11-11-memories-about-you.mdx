**2026-11-11 · Bower**

Bower's memories were meant to be about you, but a description of a product
could end up saved as a personal detail, out of sight of the colleagues who
needed it. Memories now stay about you.

## What's changed

- **Memories are about you.** Bower remembers your role, research area and
  interests, your own ways of working, such as what you call things and how
  you like records kept, and anything you ask it to remember.
- **Earlier requests count again.** Things you asked Bower to remember before
  mid-October 2026 are used again. If one stops being true, delete it in
  Settings.
- **Product and document content stays in the workspace.** What a product,
  kit, device, protocol, or document contains is no longer saved as a memory.
  Put it in workspace instructions or a workspace document, where colleagues
  can see it.
- **Methods and equipment are no longer remembered.** Memories Bower learned
  earlier about methods or equipment stay in **Settings → Preferences → What
  Bower remembers** until you delete them, marked as not used in
  conversations.
- **A detail keeps its scope.** If you label one project's samples by date,
  Bower does not assume all your projects do.
- **Bower learns only from you.** In chat, as in live mode, Bower learns from
  what you write or say, never from its own replies.

See [What Bower remembers](/ai-and-search/personal-preferences#what-bower-remembers) for the details.

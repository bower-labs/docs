**Shipped 2026-08-09 · Bird and permissions**

Guests are free, read-only members. Until now they could read and search the
workspace, but the Bird chat panel was hidden from them entirely. Now they get
it — with everything on the read side and nothing on the write side.

## What changed

A guest can open Bird and ask anything about the workspace: search across notes
and attachments, summarise a set of results, compare readings, follow a citation
trail, look things up in the open scholarly record. Answers come back with the
same inline citations everyone else gets.

Bird won't create, edit, move, delete, upload or transcribe anything for a
guest. Asked to, it says so plainly and offers to read, search or summarise
instead — rather than trying and failing.

Live agent mode stays unavailable to guests, and a guest still can't connect a
new source.

## What this costs

A guest's chats draw on the **workspace's shared chat allowance**, the same one
paid seats use — see [AI usage allowances](/workspaces-and-account/billing#ai-usage-allowances).
Guest usage doesn't touch the live-agent, transcription or extraction
allowances, because guests can't use those.

## Why

An external collaborator or stakeholder reading your workspace has the same
questions a paid member does — *what changed this week?*, *what did that batch
show?* — and answering them was work someone else had to do. Making the
assistant read-only rather than absent is the version of the feature that
matches what a guest is for.

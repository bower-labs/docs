**Shipped 2026-10-13 · Workspaces**

The workspace **Owner** can now delete a workspace themselves. Nothing is
erased straight away: the workspace is locked for 30 days, and the Owner can
restore it, unchanged, at any point in that window.

## What's new

- **Delete workspace in Settings > Workspace.** The Owner types the
  workspace's name to confirm. Admins and Members see the button dimmed, with
  the Owner's name, so they know who to ask.
- **Locked for everyone straight away.** The workspace leaves every member's
  workspace menu, members are moved to another workspace they belong to, and
  share links and pending invitations stop working.
- **Restore for 30 days.** Deleted workspaces are listed under **Scheduled for
  deletion** in **Settings > Workspace**, each with its date. Click **Restore**
  and everyone has access again.
- **Your only workspace too.** If it's the last workspace you have, Bower asks
  you to name a new one first, so you're never left without one.

## Notes

- **Billing stops renewing** when you delete the workspace, and the plan stays
  paid until the end of the current period. Restoring turns renewal back on,
  unless you had already canceled the plan yourself.
- After 30 days, everything in the workspace is permanently deleted. A record
  that it existed and was deleted is kept for compliance, without the content
  of your notes, file names, or titles.

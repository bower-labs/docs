**Shipped 2026-08-07 · Mobile**

Sometimes you open Capture to record an observation, and what you actually want is to talk it through. The Capture tab now offers both.

## What's new

- **Go live with Bird, from Capture** — in **Voice** and **Video** mode, a card below the capture ring starts a [live agent session](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode) instead of a one-off capture. A capture gives you a note or an attachment; a live session gives you a conversation.
- **Video mode starts with the camera on** — go live from Video and the camera is running as soon as the session connects, so you can point and narrate without hunting for a control. From Voice mode the session starts audio-only, and you can turn the camera on later.
- **The Bird tab is now Chat** — same tab, same place, clearer name, and it matches what the desktop sidebar has always called it.
- **Filter workspace search** — tap the filter button inside the search field on the **Workspace** tab to narrow results to a single type: protocols, attachments, collections, and so on. It's the same filter you already have in the [command palette](/ai-and-search/command-palette).

The capture ring also now sits properly centred on the screen whatever size your phone is, and the Capture tab no longer loads a recent-items list you were scrolling past to get to it.

## Try it

Open **Capture** on your phone, pick **Voice** or **Video**, and tap **Go live with Bird**.

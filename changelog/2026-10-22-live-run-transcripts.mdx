**Rolling out from 2026-10-22 · Live**

When you run a protocol in Live, Bower can now keep a transcript of the whole
spoken conversation, your lines and Bower's, as a note on the run. It's off
until you turn it on, and is reaching workspaces gradually.

## What's new

- **Your choice, remembered.** Turn on the **Transcript** switch during a
  run, and Bower keeps it on for your next runs. Turn it off to pause saving;
  the transcript marks where it was paused.
- **A workspace limit.** Admins and owners can turn off **Live run
  transcripts** in **Settings > Workspace** for everyone in the workspace.
- **A note on the run.** Open it from **View transcript** in the run record's
  **Summary**. It's searchable and editable like any note.
- **Your words, tidied lightly.** After the run, Bower fixes punctuation,
  capitals and filler words in your lines only. **View original transcript**
  in the note's menu shows them untouched.

## Notes

- No extra audio is recorded; the transcript comes from Live's own speech
  recognition.
- Anyone who can see the note can read the transcript.

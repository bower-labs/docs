**2026-11-13 · Trust & compliance**

Bower's [electronic signatures](/organisation/electronic-signatures) come with controls that support labs working under FDA 21 CFR Part 11. This page lists each control and the part of the regulation it relates to.

Bower is not certified for Part 11, and we don't describe it as Part 11 compliant. Compliance depends on how your organization validates, configures and uses a system, not on the software alone.

## The controls

| Control | What Bower does | Part 11 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Signature manifestation** | Each signature shows the signer's printed name, the date and time, and its meaning, such as **Performed by**, **Reviewed by** or **Witnessed by**, on the record and in its [export](/organisation/electronic-signatures#export-a-signed-record). | 11.50 |
| **Signature linked to its record** | A signature covers a fingerprint of the exact content signed, including the bytes of each file. A signed record is locked, and Bower shows [what changed](/organisation/electronic-signatures#see-what-was-signed) if anything is added after signing. | 11.70 |
| **Password at every signing** | Every signature, withdrawal, rejection and unlock asks for your [Bower password](/organisation/electronic-signatures#your-bower-password), checked each time for the signed-in account. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, Bower asks you to sign in with them again before it saves a new Bower password. | 11.200(a) |
| **Attempted misuse is flagged** | Repeated wrong passwords [lock signing](/organisation/electronic-signatures#if-your-password-is-refused-several-times) for a while, and the workspace's Owners and Admins are told. | 11.300(d) |
| **Only authorized people sign** | Owners and Admins grant **Can sign** and **Can review**, and confirm each person's identity before the grant. Bower records who confirmed it and when. See [Choose who can sign](/organisation/electronic-signatures#choose-who-can-sign). | 11.10(g), 11.100(b) |
| **Signing in order** | The performer signs first, and a reviewer or witness signs after. The performer can't also review or witness the same record. | 11.10(f) |
| **Reasons on every reversal** | Withdrawing, rejecting and unlocking each need a reason, and earlier signatures stay in the record's history as withdrawn or superseded. See [Withdraw, reject or unlock](/organisation/electronic-signatures#withdraw-reject-or-unlock). | 11.10(e) |
| **An audit trail you can check** | The [audit log](/trust-center/audit-logs) records who did what, when and to which record, with before and after values. Events are sealed into a hash chain that Bower checks daily, so a changed, removed or reordered event shows up. | 11.10(e) |
| **Copies for inspection** | A signed record exports as a printable PDF and an HTML page, with every file as uploaded, a manifest of SHA-256 fingerprints and instructions for checking it without Bower. | 11.10(b) |

## What stays with your organization

Part 11 also asks things of the organization that uses a system. These are yours to put in place:

- validating Bower for your intended use;
- written procedures, training and policies that hold people accountable for their electronic signatures;
- deciding who gets a signing privilege, and verifying their identity before you grant it;
- certifying to the FDA that your electronic signatures are the legally binding equivalent of handwritten ones.

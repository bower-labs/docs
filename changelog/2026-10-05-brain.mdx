**Shipped 2026-10-05 · Organization**

The page that maps how your notes, protocols, attachments, equipment,
collections and people connect is now called **Brain**. It shows the same
thing it always has. What changed is the name and where you open it.

## What's new

- **Open Brain from your avatar menu.** Click your avatar at the bottom of the
  left rail and choose **Brain**, just above **Settings**. The icon at the end
  of the Workspace panel's filter chips has been removed.
- **Show in Brain.** The action that read **Show in graph** in note, protocol,
  spreadsheet, attachment and collection menus now reads **Show in Brain**. It
  still opens Brain centered on that item. The link at the foot of an item's
  linked-items list reads **Show in Brain** too.
- **Command palette.** The **Go to** entry is now **Brain**. Typing `graph`,
  `knowledge graph`, `map` or `connections` still finds it.
- **Pricing.** The plan comparison row now reads **Brain: 2D + 3D knowledge
  graph**. It's included on every plan, as before.

## Notes

- Nothing about the graph itself has changed: the same nodes, filters, 2D and
  3D views, list view, and permission filtering.
- Bookmarks and links to the page keep working.

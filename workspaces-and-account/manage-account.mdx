## Add your ORCID iD

If you're a researcher with an [ORCID iD](https://orcid.org), saving it on your profile lets Bower answer "what have I published recently?" without you needing to share the iD each turn.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings > Profile and find the Research identity section" />
  <Step title="Type your ORCID iD in the canonical 16-digit form (e.g. 0000-0001-2345-6789)" />
  <Step title="Click Save — Bower verifies the iD against ORCID's public API and shows your name back to confirm" />
</Steps>

We only ever read your **public** ORCID profile — your name and the works you've listed publicly. We never access anything from your private ORCID record.

To remove a saved iD, clear the field and click Save. Bower will stop pulling your published works automatically.

<Note>
Your ORCID iD is also part of the onboarding flow for new accounts. You can skip it during onboarding and add it later from Profile.
</Note>

## Transfer workspace ownership

Every workspace has exactly one **Owner**, and the Owner is also the workspace's **billing owner**. To hand the workspace to someone else, for example when a lab head moves on or Bower set the workspace up for you, the current Owner transfers ownership.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Members">
    The **Transfer ownership** button sits at the top of **Active members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Transfer ownership and pick the new Owner">
    You can pick anyone already in the workspace as an Admin or Member. Guests appear in the list but can't be picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type TRANSFER to confirm">
    Type `TRANSFER` in the box and click **Transfer ownership**. The transfer happens as soon as you confirm. The new Owner doesn't need to accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What changes when ownership moves

- **The new Owner** becomes the billing owner. Invoices and billing emails go to them from now on, and they're the only person who can transfer ownership again.
- **You** become an **Admin**. You keep access to the workspace and its content.
- **The payment method on file stays the same.** If the new Owner wants to pay with their own card, they update it in **Settings > Billing**. Your plan, seats, and billing history carry over unchanged.

### Who can transfer ownership?

Only the current Owner. Everyone else sees the **Transfer ownership** button greyed out; hover over it (or focus it with the keyboard) to see who the Owner is, so you know who to ask.

### Why can't I pick someone?

- **They're a Guest.** Guests are free, read-only seats, and the Owner holds a paid seat. Remove them and re-invite them as a Member first (see [Invite team members](/workspaces-and-account/invite-team-members#paid--guest-is-remove-and-re-invite-not-in-place)), then transfer.
- **The workspace has an overdue payment.** Update the payment method in **Settings > Billing** first, so nobody inherits a failed invoice.

You can't make someone the Owner by changing their role in the member list. **Transfer ownership** is the only way, because ownership carries the billing account with it.

## Change your email or password

Email and password changes are managed from **Settings > Security**. You'll be asked to verify your current password before making changes.

## Sign in with Google or Microsoft

On the sign-in page, choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**. Complete the provider's sign-in window and Bower returns you to the app.

If you already use an email-and-password Bower account with the same email address, Bower asks you to sign in with that password first. It then links the Google or Microsoft sign-in method to the same account, so your workspaces and notes stay in one place.

When you join a workspace by invitation, sign in with the email address that received the invitation. The invitation is tied to that address.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The provider window does not open">
    Allow pop-ups for Bower in your browser, then choose the provider again. If your browser blocks the window after an earlier attempt, reload the sign-in page before retrying.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You used the wrong Google or Microsoft account">
    Sign out of Bower, then choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** again. Select the account whose email address you want to use with Bower.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy controls

Your privacy preferences are in **Settings > Security > Privacy**.

### Analytics cookies

You can control whether Bower collects anonymous usage data. The toggle in the Privacy section lets you enable or disable analytics cookies at any time. When you disable analytics, the page reloads to clear any tracking scripts that were already loaded.

In the EU, the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, this is the choice you made on the cookie banner. Elsewhere, analytics are on by default until you switch them off here. For details on what is and isn't tracked, see the [cookie policy](/trust-center/cookies).

### Export your data

Click **Export** in the Privacy section to download a JSON file containing all your personal data: your profile, workspace memberships, projects, artifacts, and audit history across all your workspaces.

Data exports are rate-limited to one request per 24 hours.

## Session management

### Sign out

Go to **Settings > Security > Account Access** and click **Sign out** to end your session on the current device. Other devices remain signed in.

### Revoke all sessions

If you suspect unauthorised access or have lost a device, click **Revoke all** in the Account Access section. This invalidates every active session across all devices immediately. You will be signed out and need to sign in again.

### Automatic idle timeout

Bower automatically signs you out after 30 minutes of inactivity. A warning appears 2 minutes before the timeout, giving you the option to stay signed in.

## Delete your account

<Warning>
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We recommend exporting your data first.
</Warning>

To delete your account, go to **Settings > Security > Danger Zone** and click **Delete my account**. You'll be asked to type "DELETE" to confirm.

**What gets deleted:**
- Your profile (email, name) and authentication record
- Your workspace memberships and OAuth integrations
- Workspaces where you are the sole member, including all content and files

**What gets kept:**
- In shared workspaces, your notes, files, and collections are preserved for the team.
- Audit logs are anonymized: the record of actions is kept, but your identity is removed.

If you own a workspace that other people use, Bower asks you to [transfer ownership](#transfer-workspace-ownership) to one of them before your account can be deleted. If no one in it can take ownership (you're alone, or everyone else is a Guest), you can delete that workspace instead to finish deleting your account.

A workspace you've already [deleted](/workspaces-and-account/delete-workspace) and that's waiting out its 30 days doesn't block account deletion. It's still permanently deleted on its date, and nobody can restore it once your account is gone.

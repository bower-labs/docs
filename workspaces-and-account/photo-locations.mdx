---

A photo can carry the place it was taken: the camera's GPS position, or for a [Live](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode) capture, your phone's location. **Show where photos were taken** decides whether anyone in the workspace sees it.

## Can I hide where my photos were taken?

Yes. A workspace owner or admin can turn it off for the whole workspace:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings > Workspace" />
  <Step title="Find Privacy and compliance" />
  <Step title="Turn off Show where photos were taken" />
</Steps>

The switch is on by default. Members see it but can't change it. It applies to every photo in the workspace, not one at a time.

## What the setting changes

| | On | Off |
|---|---|---|
| Location pin under a photo's title | Shown | Hidden |
| Bower, the in-app assistant | Sees the location | Doesn't see it |
| Asking Bower for photos by place | Works | Doesn't work |
| [AI apps connected to Bower](/integrations/ai-app-access) | Never given it | Never given it |

Turning the switch off hides locations, it doesn't delete them. Turn it back on and they reappear.

## Do downloaded photos include the location?

It depends on when the photo was added. Bower now stores new photos without location data in the file itself, so downloads and [share links](/organisation/sharing-artifacts) of those photos don't carry it.

Photos added before this change keep the location inside their original file. Hiding locations in the app doesn't remove it from those files, so a download or share link of an older photo can still include where it was taken.

---
title: "Switch workspaces"
description: "Move between workspaces. Each one is fully isolated."
---

If you belong to more than one workspace, you can switch between them at any time.

## How to switch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account menu">
    Click your avatar at the bottom of the left rail, then **Switch workspace**. The current workspace's name is shown under it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a workspace">
    Your workspaces are listed in alphabetical order, with a check beside the one you're in. Click the one you want.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everything you then see, including notes, collections, members and settings, belongs to that workspace only.

## Workspace logos

Each workspace in the list shows its logo next to its name, so you can find the right one at a glance. A workspace without a logo shows its initials instead.

Workspace owners and admins can add a logo:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open workspace settings">
    Go to **Settings > Workspace**. The workspace's tile sits beside its name, showing its initials until it has a logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an image">
    Click the tile. In the window that opens, drop a PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG image of up to 5 MB, or click to choose one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust and save">
    Drag to position the logo, zoom, or rotate it, then click **Save logo**. A wide logo starts fitted whole. A logo with a see-through background stays see-through when it reads in both light and dark mode. Otherwise it is placed on white (or on dark grey if the logo itself is light), so it never disappears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everyone in the workspace sees the new logo. To change it, click the tile and add a new image; to go back to initials, click the tile and choose **Remove logo**.

## Workspace isolation

Nothing crosses between workspaces unless you move or copy it:

- **Notes** captured in Workspace A are not visible in Workspace B.
- **Search** covers only the current workspace.
- **Bower** draws only on the workspace you're in.
- **Members** of Workspace A have no access to Workspace B unless they're invited to it.

## Switching on your phone

In the [Bower app](/getting-started/mobile), choose a workspace at the top of the **Dashboard** tab, or tap the title of the **Workspace** tab. Everything you capture, including from [Bower Glasses](/bower-glasses/overview), saves to the workspace you've chosen.

In your [phone's browser](/getting-started/install-pwa), use the workspace dropdown at the top of the **Capture** tab, or go to **Settings > Workspace > Current workspace**.

In a live session, Bower sees through the glasses camera, hears you, and answers through the speakers. Everything else about [live agent mode](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode) works the same as on your phone.

## Start a live session

- Press and hold the glasses button.
- Say *"Go live"* or *"Start live"*.
- In the Bower app, open a protocol and tap **Execute Protocol** to go live already running it.

The live screen stays open in the Bower app on your phone while you work.

The live screen has a **Stream** and **Capture** switch. **Stream**, the default, lets Bower see the camera feed. In **Capture**, Bower doesn't see the feed, but snapshots and clips are full-resolution photos and video from the glasses.

If the glasses aren't on Wi-Fi yet, the session starts voice only and adds video once they connect. In **Offline mode**, Live uses your phone's mobile data: Bower hears you but can't see, and takes photos through your glasses.

To end the session, press and hold the button again.

## Run a protocol

Ask Bower to run a protocol, or start it with **Execute Protocol**. Bower reads each step and moves on when you tell it you're done:

- *"Run the buffer prep protocol"*
- *"Next step"* · *"Mark this done"* · *"Go back"*
- *"Go to step 3"* jumps straight there. If you name a step by its title instead, Bower reads back the step it found and waits for a *"yes"*.

The step cards, timers, and deviations work as described in [execution mode](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode#execution-mode-running-a-protocol).

## Capture during a session

- *"Take a snapshot"* saves what the glasses see.
- *"Make a note that the sample looked cloudy"* creates a note.
- *"Start recording"* and *"Stop recording"* save a clip of up to 2 minutes.

Captures from the session are grouped in a collection for it, and during a protocol run they're linked to the protocol.

## What shows on your phone

Bower Glasses have no display, so two things appear on the live screen in the Bower app instead:

- The **REC** indicator, while a clip is recording. In a background *"Hey Bower"* session the live screen isn't open, so there's no indicator.
- Anything you ask Bower to show, such as *"show me the video for this protocol"*.

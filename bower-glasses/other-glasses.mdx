Bower Glasses on Mentra Live are where we focus. Bower's live agent also runs on two other pairs of smart glasses.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Meta Ray-Ban">
    No display, so replies are spoken and kept short. You can run protocols, ask questions, take snapshots, make notes, and record clips by voice. Commands that show something on a screen aren't available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="VITURE Luma Ultra">
    A heads-up display shows the current protocol step and an in-world panel for images and reference videos. Beyond the phone commands, you can move the display (*"Pin it here"*, *"Follow me"*, *"Bring it closer"*), resize it with hand gestures, put it to sleep (*"Go to standby"*), and quit (*"Quit Bower"*, confirmed on screen).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

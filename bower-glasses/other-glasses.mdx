Bower Glasses on Mentra Live are where we focus. Bower's live agent also runs on VITURE Luma Ultra smart glasses.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="VITURE Luma Ultra">

Beyond the phone commands, you can move the display (*"Pin it here"*, *"Follow me"*, *"Bring it closer"*), resize it with hand gestures, put it to sleep (*"Go to standby"*), and quit (*"Quit Bower"*, confirmed on screen).

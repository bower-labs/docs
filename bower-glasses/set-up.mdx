You set up Bower Glasses once, in the Bower app on your phone. If you don't have the app yet, see [Bower on your phone](/getting-started/mobile).

## Pair and set up

Turn the glasses on and put them in pairing mode, then open the **Devices** tab in the Bower app. Tap your glasses when they appear in the list. The first time you connect a pair, Bower walks you through setup:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bluetooth">
    Pair the glasses with your phone. On Android, allow Bluetooth and Location when asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wi-Fi">
    Tap **Scan for networks**, choose your network, and tap **Join**. The glasses need a network to capture, so you can't skip this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Voice">
    Tap **Enable voice commands** to download the speech model, about 75 MB, once. Voice commands are recognized on your phone. You can skip this step and turn voice commands on later in **Settings → Communication**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Firmware">
    Bower checks for a glasses update and installs it. Keep the screen open until it finishes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name">
    Give the glasses a nickname if you like, then tap **Start Capturing**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the first time, the glasses reconnect without repeating setup.

## Hear Bower in your glasses on iPhone

iPhone connects the glasses' speakers separately from the glasses themselves. Until you pair them once, Bower speaks from your phone. On the **Devices** tab, tap **Glasses audio** and follow the guide:

1. Keep your glasses on and connected.
2. Open the iPhone **Settings** app and tap **Bluetooth**.
3. Under **Other Devices**, tap the name that starts with "Mentra Live".
4. Come back to the Bower app. The screen updates on its own.

If "Mentra Live" isn't in the list, start a live session with your glasses and check again. When it's working, the row reads **On · Bower speaks through your glasses**.

## Choose a workspace

Hands-free captures save to the workspace selected on the **Dashboard** tab. Check it before you start, especially if you belong to more than one workspace.

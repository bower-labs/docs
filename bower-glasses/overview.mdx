Bower Glasses let you capture and talk to Bower without putting anything down. They run on Mentra Live smart glasses: a camera, microphones, and speakers in a pair of frames, with no display. The glasses pair with the Bower app on your phone, and everything they capture lands in your workspace.

<GlassesVisualGuide />

## What you can do

- **Capture hands-free.** Press the button or say *"Take a photo"* or *"Start recording"*. Photos, video, and voice memos save to your workspace. See [Capture hands-free](/bower-glasses/capture).
- **Talk to Bower live.** Press and hold the button or say *"Go live"*. Bower sees what you see, hears you, and answers through the speakers. See [Live and protocols](/bower-glasses/live-and-protocols).
- **Run a protocol.** Bower reads each step and moves on when you say you're done, and anything you capture during the run is linked to it.

## What you need

- **Mentra Live glasses.** In the Bower app they appear as **Bower Glasses**.
- **The Bower app** on your phone, signed in to your workspace.
- **A Wi-Fi network** the glasses can join. Photos and video normally travel over Wi-Fi.

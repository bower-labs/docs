Bower's [live agent mode](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode) is voice-driven, but **what you can ask for depends on the device**. Your phone has a screen and a touchscreen. [Bower Glasses](/getting-started/bower-glasses) have a camera, microphones, and speakers but no display, so anything Bower shows you opens on your phone. This page is the quick reference for which commands work where.

You don't need to memorize exact phrases. Bower understands intent, so paraphrases of the examples below work too. The phrases are just reliable starting points.

## At a glance

| What you want to do | Phone | Bower Glasses |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| Run a protocol and step through it by voice | ✓ | ✓ |
| Ask questions, talk through your work | ✓ | ✓ |
| Take a snapshot or create a note | ✓ | ✓ |
| Start and stop recording a clip | ✓ | ✓ (REC shows in the Bower app) |
| **Show** an image or reference video | ✓ (overlay) | ✓ (on your phone) |
| Capture without going live | | ✓ (*"Take a photo"*, *"Start recording"*) |

<Note>
**Jumping to a specific step is confirmed first.** On any device, when you ask to jump to a named or numbered step, such as *"skip to step 3"* or *"go to the vortex step"*, Bower reads the step's title back (*"Jump to 'Vortex the sample', that right?"*) and waits for a *"yes"* before moving. Marking a step done, skipping, and going back stay instant. See [execution mode](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode#execution-mode-running-a-protocol) for details.
</Note>

## Commands by device

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Bower Glasses">
    Bower Glasses listen for commands whenever the Bower app is open, even before you go live. Voice commands need to be turned on during [setup](/getting-started/bower-glasses#set-up-your-glasses) or in **Settings → Communication**.

    **Capture without going live**
    - *"Take a photo"* · *"Take a picture"*
    - *"Start recording"* / *"Stop recording"*
    - *"Start voice memo"* / *"Stop microphone"*

    **Go live**
    - *"Go live"* · *"Start live"*
    - *"Hey Bower"*, then ask your question

    **In a live session**
    - *"Run the buffer prep protocol"* · *"Next step"* · *"Mark this done"* · *"Go back"*
    - *"Take a snapshot"* · *"Make a note that the sample looked cloudy"*
    - *"Start recording"* / *"Stop recording"*. The **REC** indicator shows on the live screen in the Bower app.
    - *"Show me the video for this protocol"* opens on your phone
    - Ask anything: *"How much sodium chloride does this step need?"*, *"Is 4 °C okay for this?"*

    You can also use the glasses button: press for a photo, double-press for video, press and hold to go live. See [Bower Glasses](/getting-started/bower-glasses#capture-with-the-button).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Phone">
    Live mode in the Bower app or in Bower chat in your phone's browser: voice plus an on-screen overlay and your phone's camera. See [Live agent mode](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode) for the full walkthrough.

    **Run and step through a protocol**
    - *"Run the PCR setup protocol"* · *"Next step"* · *"Mark this done"* · *"Skip to step 3"*

    **Show media on screen**
    - *"Show me the gel from last week"* (or any saved image) appears as an overlay
    - *"Show me the video for this protocol"* · *"Skip to step 2"* to seek it
    - Close the overlay by **tapping the ×** (or tapping outside it)

    **Capture and record**
    - *"Take a snapshot"* saves the current camera frame
    - *"Create a note from what we just discussed"*
    - *"Start recording"* / *"Stop recording"* saves a short clip ([details](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode#recording-video-clips))

    **Video awareness**
    - With video on, point your camera and ask: *"What's the reading on that display?"*
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Other glasses

Bower's live agent also runs on two other pairs of smart glasses.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Meta Ray-Ban">
    No display, so replies are spoken and kept short. You can run protocols, ask questions, take snapshots, make notes, and record clips by voice. Commands that show something on a screen aren't available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="VITURE Luma Ultra">
    A heads-up display shows the current protocol step and an in-world panel for images and reference videos. Beyond the phone commands, you can move the display (*"Pin it here"*, *"Follow me"*, *"Bring it closer"*), resize it by pinching with both hands, put it to sleep (*"Go to standby"*), and quit (*"Quit Bower"*, confirmed on screen).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

This is the one list of what you can say to Bower, and what the Bower Glasses button does. Other pages link here rather than repeating it.

There are two kinds of commands:

- **Bower Glasses commands** work while you're not in a live session. Your phone recognizes them, so say them close to the way they're written below.
- **Live commands** work in a [live session](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode) on any device. Bower understands what you mean, so you don't need the exact words. The phrases below are reliable starting points.

## Bower Glasses, outside a live session

Voice commands are **off until you turn them on**: tap **Enable voice commands** during [setup](/bower-glasses/set-up), or turn on **Idle voice commands** in **Profile → Settings → Communication**. The first time, your phone downloads a speech model of about 75 MB. Commands work while the Bower app is open on your phone, not while it's in the background.

| Say | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| *"Take a photo"* · *"Take a picture"* · *"Take a snapshot"* | Takes a photo. |
| *"Start recording"* · *"Take a video"* · *"Record a video"* | Starts recording video. |
| *"Stop recording"* · *"Stop the video"* | Stops the video, or the voice note if one is recording. |
| *"Take a voice note"* · *"Start voice memo"* | Starts an audio recording. |
| *"Stop the note"* · *"Stop voice memo"* | Stops and saves the audio recording. |
| *"Go live"* · *"Start live"* | Starts a [live session](/bower-glasses/live-and-protocols). |
| *"Hey Bower"*, then your question | Bower answers out loud in a background conversation, which ends after 3 minutes without activity. |

While a voice note records, only the stop phrases count, so you can say anything else into it.

<Note>
Hearing **"Bower"** on its own is enough to start a *"Hey Bower"* conversation, so saying the name near your glasses can wake it. With the glasses disconnected, *"Hey Bower"* still works through your phone's microphone, and the capture commands don't.
</Note>

### The glasses button

| Press | Outside a live session | In a live session |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Press** | Takes a photo. Stops a video that's recording. | Shows **Capture busy**. Say *"Take a photo"* instead. |
| **Double-press** | Starts recording video. Press again to stop. | Shows **Capture busy**. Say *"Start recording"* instead. |
| **Press and hold** | Starts a live session, or ends a *"Hey Bower"* conversation. | Ends the session straight away. |

## In any live session

These work in the Bower app, in your phone's browser, and on Bower Glasses.

| Say | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| *"Take a photo"* · *"Take a snapshot"* | Saves what the camera sees. |
| *"Record this"* · *"Start recording"* | Records a clip. See [Recording video clips](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode#recording-video-clips). |
| *"Stop recording"* · *"End the recording"* | Stops the clip. Say the word "recording": a bare *"stop"* or *"that's enough"* doesn't end it, because people say those while filming. |
| *"Note, the pellet is loose"* · *"Make a note that the buffer was cold"* | Saves what you said after the word "note", in your own words, as a note. Start with the note request. *"Take a note"* on its own makes Bower ask what to write. |
| *"Make a note summarizing what we discussed"* | Bower writes the note for you. |
| *"Show me the gel from last week"* · *"Show me the video for this protocol"* | Shows it on your phone. |
| *"Let me know when the gel turns pink"* | Sets a [visual watcher](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode#visual-watchers). |

Anything else, you can just ask: *"What's the reading on that display?"*, *"Is 4 °C okay for this?"*

On Bower Glasses, the outside-a-session commands are off during a live session, with one exception: if Bower can't be reached for a few seconds, your phone still takes a photo or starts and stops a recording when you ask.

### Standby (phone browser)

| Say | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| *"Go to standby"* · *"Standby mode"* · *"Go to sleep"* · *"Goodbye Bower"* | Puts Bower in [standby](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode#standby-mode). |
| *"Hey Bower"* · *"Hello Bower"* · *"Hi Bower"* | Wakes Bower. |

A standby phrase works as a short command, not inside a longer sentence.

## Running a protocol

| Say | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| *"Run the buffer prep protocol"* | Starts the run. |
| *"Done"* · *"Next"* · *"Finished that"* | Marks the step done straight away. |
| *"Skip this one"* | Skips the step. |
| *"Go back"* · *"I wasn't finished"* | Reopens the previous step. |
| *"Go to step 5"* | Moves straight to step 5. |
| *"Go back to the staining step"* | Reads back the step it found and moves only after you say *"yes"*. |
| *"Pause"* · *"Hold on"* | Stops the run clock. Bower stays quiet. |
| *"Resume"* | Reads the current step again and carries on. |
| *"What's next?"* · *"Which step is this?"* | Tells you, without changing anything. |
| *"I used 50 µL, not 100"* | Records a [deviation](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode#reporting-deviations) on the current step. |
| *"End the protocol"* · *"We're finished"* | Ends the run once every step is done. With steps still open, Bower says how many and keeps the run going. |
| *"Start over"* | Starts the protocol again from step 1. If steps are already done, Bower asks first. |
| *"Go back"* or *"Undo"* just after the last step | Reopens a finished run. See [Undoing the last step](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode#undoing-the-last-step). |

Bower can't time steps yet, so use your phone's timer for incubations and waits.

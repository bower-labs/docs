Bower's [live agent mode](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode) is voice-driven, but **what you can ask for depends on the device**. Your phone has a screen and a touchscreen. [Bower Glasses](/bower-glasses/overview) have a camera, microphones, and speakers but no display, so anything Bower shows you opens on your phone. This page is the quick reference for which commands work where.

You don't need to memorize exact phrases. Bower understands intent, so paraphrases of the examples below work too. The phrases are just reliable starting points.

## At a glance

| What you want to do | Phone | Bower Glasses |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| Run a protocol and step through it by voice | ✓ | ✓ |
| Ask questions, talk through your work | ✓ | ✓ |
| Take a snapshot or create a note | ✓ | ✓ |
| Start and stop recording a clip | ✓ | ✓ (REC shows on the Bower app's live screen) |
| **Show** an image or reference video | ✓ (overlay) | ✓ (on your phone) |
| Capture without going live | | ✓ (*"Take a photo"*, *"Start recording"*) |

<Note>
**Jumping to a step by name is confirmed first.** On any device, *"go to step 3"* jumps straight there. When you name a step instead, such as *"go to the vortex step"*, Bower reads back the step it found and waits for a *"yes"* before moving. Marking a step done, skipping, and going back stay instant. See [execution mode](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode#execution-mode-running-a-protocol) for details.
</Note>

## Commands by device

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Bower Glasses">
    Bower Glasses listen for commands whenever the Bower app is open, even before you go live. Turn them on during [setup](/bower-glasses/set-up) or with **Idle voice commands** in **Profile → Settings → Communication**.

    **Capture without going live**
    - *"Take a photo"* · *"Take a picture"*
    - *"Start recording"* / *"Stop recording"*
    - *"Start voice memo"* / *"Stop microphone"*

    **Go live**
    - *"Go live"* · *"Start live"*
    - *"Hey Bower"*, then ask your question

    **In a live session**
    - *"Run the buffer prep protocol"* · *"Next step"* · *"Mark this done"* · *"Go back"*
    - *"Take a snapshot"* · *"Make a note that the sample looked cloudy"*
    - *"Start recording"* / *"Stop recording"*. The **REC** indicator shows on the live screen in the Bower app when it's open.
    - *"Show me the video for this protocol"* opens on your phone
    - Ask anything: *"How much sodium chloride does this step need?"*, *"Is 4 °C okay for this?"*

    You can also use the glasses button: press for a photo, double-press for video, press and hold to go live. See [Capture hands-free](/bower-glasses/capture).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Phone">
    Live mode in the Bower app or in Bower chat in your phone's browser: voice plus an on-screen overlay and your phone's camera. See [Live agent mode](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode) for the full walkthrough.

    **Run and step through a protocol**
    - *"Run the PCR setup protocol"* · *"Next step"* · *"Mark this done"* · *"Skip to step 3"*

    **Show media on screen**
    - *"Show me the gel from last week"* (or any saved image) appears as an overlay
    - *"Show me the video for this protocol"* · *"Skip to step 2"* to seek it
    - Close the overlay by **tapping the ×** (or tapping outside it)

    **Capture and record**
    - *"Take a snapshot"* saves the current camera frame
    - *"Create a note from what we just discussed"*
    - *"Start recording"* / *"Stop recording"* saves a short clip ([details](/ai-and-search/live-agent-mode#recording-video-clips)). To stop, say the word "recording": a bare "stop" or "that's enough" doesn't end the clip, because people say those while filming.

    **Video awareness**
    - With video on, point your camera and ask: *"What's the reading on that display?"*
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

For glasses from other makers, see [Other glasses](/bower-glasses/other-glasses).

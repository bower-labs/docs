Live agent mode is a spoken conversation with Bower while you work. Talk through an experiment, ask follow-up questions, or run a protocol step by step without typing. With video on, Bower sees what your camera sees, so you can ask *"What's the reading on that display?"* or *"Does this sample look contaminated to you?"* Bower responds to what it sees only when you ask, or when you've set a [visual watcher](#visual-watchers).

Live runs on your phone and on [Bower Glasses](/bower-glasses/overview), not on a computer.

## How to start a live session

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Bower app">
    Tap **+** in the tab bar and turn the dial to **Live**. To run a protocol, open it and tap **Execute Protocol** instead.

    Allow microphone and camera access when asked, then start talking.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bower Glasses">
    Press and hold the glasses button, or say *"Go live"*. See [Live and protocols with Bower Glasses](/bower-glasses/live-and-protocols).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Phone browser">
    In Bower chat, tap the waveform button beside the send (arrow) button. Its tooltip reads **Live conversation**.

    You can also tap **Go live with Bower** on the **Capture** tab in voice or video mode.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
On a computer, get the [Bower app](/getting-started/mobile) on your phone and start Live from its capture menu.
</Note>

## Capturing during a session

Bower saves something to your workspace only when you ask it to or take a photo yourself.

| Say or do | What is saved |
| --- | --- |
| *"Take a snapshot"* | The current camera frame, as an attachment |
| *"Create a note from what we just discussed"* | A note with the key points of the conversation |
| Tap **Take a photo** (app) or **Take photo** (browser) | A full camera frame, sharper than what Bower sees in conversation |

Everything you capture is searchable straight away.

You can also ask Bower to [enter values in a spreadsheet](/organisation/spreadsheets#live-agent-mode).

### Taking a photo yourself

In a phone-browser session, the round **Take photo** button sits above the controls while your camera is on, and hides in standby. It shows a spinner while the photo saves, then a check mark. If it fails, you'll see "Photo didn't save. Try again." and nothing is added.

During a protocol run, the photo is linked to the run. Bower knows you took it, and at which step, without stopping to comment, so you can later ask *"which step was that last photo for?"*

## Recording video clips

Say *"Record this"*, *"Start recording"* or *"Film this for me"*. Bower replies "Recording. Say stop recording when you're done." and a red **REC** indicator stays on screen while it records. Ask to start again while it records and Bower tells you it is already recording.

To stop, say *"Stop recording"* or *"End the recording"*. The word "recording" is required: a bare "stop", "cut" or "that's enough" doesn't end the clip, because people say those while filming. Bower replies "Recording stopped." and tells you if the clip doesn't save, or if it stays on your glasses to come over after the session. Phrases such as *"recall the protocol"* or *"what's the record temperature"* don't start one.

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum length | **2 minutes**; Bower stops automatically |
| At a time | One recording per session |
| Saved to | Workspace attachments, as MP4 that plays inline |
| Storage | Counts toward your workspace storage |
| Quality | 1–2 frames per second with full-quality audio, for bench observation rather than fast motion |

If your connection drops mid-recording, Bower tries to save what it received up to the drop, but can't tell you whether it did. Check your attachments after reconnecting.

<Info>
The **REC** indicator also tells colleagues nearby they may be on camera. Bower Glasses have no display, so it shows on the live screen in the Bower app when that is open. A background *"Hey Bower"* session shows no indicator, so tell people before you record. Clips are visible to anyone who can see your other workspace attachments.
</Info>

## Standby mode

Standby is available in phone-browser sessions. Use it for a private conversation, a call, or a long session where you want Bower available but not listening.

- **To enter standby:** tap the standby button, or say **"Standby Bower"**.
- **To wake Bower:** say **"Hello Bower"**, or tap the standby button again.
- **Auto-standby:** after 30 seconds of quiet, Bower goes into standby on its own. Turn it off in **Live settings** (the cog at the top right).

In standby, Bower's servers receive no audio or video. Your browser's own speech recognition listens for "Hello Bower", so the microphone stays on; in Chrome and Edge that audio goes to Google's or Microsoft's speech service, not to Bower.

## Execution mode (running a protocol)

Say *"Run the buffer prep protocol"* (or *"Start…"*, *"Let's do…"*), or tap **Execute Protocol** in the Bower app. Bower walks you through the steps and records the run on the protocol; see [Protocol runs](/organisation/protocols#protocol-runs). If more than one protocol matches what Bower heard, it lists them and asks which.

If your workspace has [Live run transcripts](/ai-and-search/live-run-transcripts) turned on, Bower also saves the spoken conversation as a transcript note on the run.

### Step cards

Each step appears as a card at the bottom of the live screen. Swipe to look ahead or back. Tap **Mark done** to finish a step without speaking, or **Watch** to play the step in the protocol's reference video, when it has one.

Tap **Details** for the full step: formatted instructions, materials, and attached pictures, videos and files. Pictures and videos open full screen; files open in a new tab, so your run keeps going.

### Voice commands during a run

| Say | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| *"Done"*, *"Next"*, *"Finished that"* | Marks the step done straight away, without asking you to confirm |
| *"Skip this one"* | Skips the step |
| *"Jump to step 5"* | Moves straight to step 5 |
| *"Go back to the staining step"* | Reads back the step it found, such as *"Go to step 4, Set up gas lines?"*, and moves only after you say *"yes"* |
| *"Pause for a sec"*, *"Hold on"* | Stops the run clock; Bower stays quiet |
| *"Resume"*, *"Where were we?"* | Reads the current step again and carries on |
| *"We're finished"* after the last step | Ends the run |

If Bower isn't sure which step you meant, it asks rather than guessing.

### Undoing the last step

When you mark the last step done, Bower finishes the protocol. If that *"done"* was misheard, you have **10 minutes**, in the same live session, to take it back:

1. Say *"Go back"* or *"Undo"*, or name a step, such as *"Go back to step 6"*.
2. Bower asks first, for example *"Reopen the finished protocol at step 6, Image the gel?"*
3. Say *"yes"* and the run reopens at that step. Say no and it stays finished.

Only your voice can reopen a run, not a tap. A reopened run you leave unfinished is recorded as **Ended early**. After 10 minutes, or for a run that ended early, start a new run.

### Timers

Say *"Remind me in 30 minutes to take the gel out"* or *"Set a 5-minute timer"*. Bower reads the duration back so you can catch a misheard time, then speaks the reminder when it's due. A timer belongs to the current step and is cleared when you move on, so set a long timer on the step it should fire during.

### Reporting deviations

Tell Bower when you depart from the protocol: *"I used 50 µL, not 100"*, *"I waited 10 minutes instead of 30"*, *"That tube touched the bench"*. Bower records the deviation against the current step, with a category such as timing, parameter or sterile breach.

### Notes during a run

Say *"remember that we use 50 µL here"* or *"make a note that the buffer was cold"*, and Bower writes it into a note on the run and tells you so. The note appears on the [run record](/organisation/protocols#what-is-on-a-run-record) straight away. Bower doesn't bring these notes up on later runs, so if a change should apply every time, edit the protocol. *"Take a note"* on its own starts a voice note for you to dictate.

Bower no longer keeps per-step tips between runs; the **Bower AI annotations** entry has been removed from the protocol menu.

## Visual watchers

Ask Bower to react when it sees something, in any live session: *"Let me know when the gel turns pink"*, *"Alert me if my hand touches the bench"*. Bower confirms, checks the camera every few seconds, and speaks your response when it sees a clear match. Nothing is saved when a watcher fires.

- **Up to 3 watchers per session.** Clear one with *"stop watching for the gel"*.
- **About 15 seconds between repeats** of the same watcher, so a cue that stays in view doesn't fire constantly.
- **Watchers end with the session.** Set them again next time.
- **Watchers add cost while active**, so clear them when you're done.
- Ask *"what are you watching for?"* to hear the active watchers and how often each has fired.

Watchers work best with concrete, visible cues, such as *"the centrifuge display reaching 10000 rpm"*. They don't work for abstract targets (*"good things"*), your intentions (*"when I'm done"*), or a readout that isn't in view of the camera.

title: "Protocol step videos"
description: "Attach a reference video to a protocol and mark where each step happens, then ask Bower to play the right moment, hands-free."
---

A protocol tells you *what* to do. A step video shows you *how it's done*. Attach a short recording of the protocol being performed (a training video, a recording from a previous run, anything) and mark the moment each step happens, the way YouTube chapters work. From then on, anyone running the protocol can pull up exactly the right clip without scrubbing.

This matters most when your hands are full. During a live session you can simply say *"show me this step"* and the video appears, already playing from the right moment, on your phone or on glasses.

## Setting up a step video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the protocol">
    Open the protocol in your workspace. Below its steps you'll see a **Video guide** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a video">
    Click **Add video** and upload your video, or choose a video already attached to the protocol and click **Use this video**. H.264 MP4 files work best across devices. A new upload appears in the list once it has finished processing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark each step">
    Play the video. When you reach the moment a step begins, click **Set** next to that step. The timestamp is captured from wherever the video is, so pause first for precision.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Markers save automatically as you set them, so there's nothing to submit. To change one, clear it (×) and set it again. The step list is your protocol's own steps, the same ones a live run ticks through. If the page shows a version other than the one a live run uses, a note tells you which version's steps the markers follow.

### When the steps change

If you add, rename, remove or reorder steps after marking the video, the section says the protocol's steps changed since you marked it. Click **Re-sync from protocol** to review: markers carry across to the steps they match, and you set any new ones. Videos marked before your protocol had structured steps show this prompt too; re-syncing moves their markers onto the current steps. Until you re-sync, the video keeps playing in live sessions.

A protocol with more than 100 steps, or with a step that has no title, can't be run live, so its video can't be marked until that's fixed.

## Asking Bower for the video

Once a video is bound, these all work in a live session, in any mode, whether or not you're running the protocol:

- *"Show me the video for this protocol"*: plays the bound video
- *"Show me this step"*: while running the protocol, plays the current step's clip
- *"Skip to step 3"*: jumps by step number
- *"Show me the vortex step"*: jumps by what the step is called; a fragment of the name is enough
- *"Play it from the start"*: restarts the video

If a step doesn't have a marker yet, Bower will say so and list the steps that do.

## Where the video appears

- **On your phone**: an overlay above the live session view, and a **Watch** button on the current step during protocol runs
- **Bower Glasses**: on the live screen in the Bower app, since the glasses have no display
- **VITURE Luma Ultra glasses**: a floating panel beside the protocol card, so the clip and your bench stay in view together

## A few details worth knowing

- One video per protocol for now. **Replace** swaps in a different video and keeps your markers, so check them against the new recording.
- To remove a step video, click **Remove** in the Video guide section. This removes the markers *and* moves the recording to Trash, where it's restorable for 30 days. The protocol itself is untouched.
- To see every capture taken while running a protocol, choose **View runs** from the protocol's menu and open a run. Its record shows the captures beside the step they were taken for. See [Protocol runs](/organisation/protocols#protocol-runs).
- Markers are tied to your protocol's steps. If you substantially rewrite the steps, re-sync and check the markers: Bower carries them across where the steps match, but only you can say where a rewritten step now begins.

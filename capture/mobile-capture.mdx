---

In the Bower app, tap **+** in the middle of the tab bar to open the capture menu, then turn the dial to **Vision**, **Voice**, **Text**, or **Upload**. Each one ends on a review screen where you name the capture (or let Bower name it), choose where it goes, and save.

Captures save to the workspace selected on the **Dashboard** tab. Don't have the app? See [Bower on your phone](/getting-started/mobile).

## Voice

Bower records your spoken observations and transcribes them using scientific speech recognition trained on domain vocabulary. The transcript becomes an editable note, with the original recording attached.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Voice">
    Recording starts straight away, with a live waveform. Tap **Pause** and **Resume** as you need to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Stop and save">
    The review screen opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to transcribe it">
    Under **Transcribe as**, choose **One clean transcript.** for dictation, or **Labels Speaker 1, 2…** to label each speaker in a meeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    Add a title or leave it for Bower, choose a destination, and tap **Save voice note**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
**A few things worth knowing:**
- Bower recognizes scientific terminology: units, chemical names, numeric values, and Latin binomials.
- If a value looks outside an expected range, Bower will flag it after capture, not during.
- You can rate the quality of transcriptions and text extractions. Both positive and negative [feedback](/workspaces-and-account/feedback) helps us improve accuracy for scientific content.
</Info>

## Photo

Point your camera at a handwritten notebook, printed document, instrument display, or sample label. Bower reads the image, extracts the text into an editable note, and describes what's in the photo. (This is sometimes called OCR, optical character recognition.)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Vision, then Photo">
    Take a photo, or tap the gallery button to choose one from your library. Pinch or use the zoom presets to get closer, and turn on the flash if you need it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Photo note or Equipment">
    On the review screen, save it as a **Photo note**, or choose **Equipment**. Bower then reads the label to identify make, model, and serial number for a new [equipment](/organisation/equipment) item, and shows how confident the scan is. Equipment needs a connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    Add a title or leave it for Bower, choose a destination, and tap **Save photo** (or **Save to Equipment Register**).
  </Step>
</Steps>

For photos taken on your phone, Bower keeps when and where each one was taken and the device that took it. When a photo has a location, it shows as a pin with the coordinates under the title; tap or click it to open the spot in Google Maps. The rest is in the attachment's **⋮** menu. Because Bower knows where your photos were taken, you can ask it for them by place: *"Show me the photos from our field trip to Bali."*

### What Bower can read

- Printed text and tables (approximately 98% accuracy)
- Handwritten lab notebooks
- Instrument displays and gauges
- Mixed handwriting and print
- Sample storage labels

Extracted tables are inline-editable, low-confidence regions are highlighted, and everything becomes searchable immediately.

Recognized equations appear as mathematical notation in the note. Diagrams and other relevant figure regions appear inline as crops from the original photo, in reading order, rather than as AI-redrawn diagrams. If Bower cannot preserve part of the layout or a crop, it flags the note for review. The original photo remains attached as the source of truth.

## Video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Vision, then Video">
    Record a clip, or choose one from your library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    Add a title or leave it for Bower, choose a destination, and tap **Save video**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Bower keeps the video as an attachment and processes it after saving. Video captures are not currently transcribed. Files can be up to 500 MB, which is about 10 minutes of video recorded in the app.

## Text

Choose **Text**, type or paste your note, and tap **Save note**. Leave the title empty and Bower names it for you.

## Upload

Choose **Upload** to send a file from your phone: images, video, audio, PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or CSV, up to 500 MB. Bower reads the file and files it in your workspace.

## Where captures go

On the review screen, tap **Add to collection…** to choose where the capture goes: the workspace itself, an existing collection, or a **New collection**. The app remembers your last choice for each workspace.

To add a capture to an existing note or protocol, open it and, under **Attach capture**, tap **Photo / video** or **Voice**. Bower keeps the original as an attachment. For a voice note or photo, it also adds the transcript or extracted text.

## What happens after capture

When you tap save, the app keeps the capture on your phone and takes you to the **Workspace** tab while it uploads in the background. Bower then transcribes or reads it, and names it if you left the title empty.

If you're offline, the capture waits on your phone. A banner at the top of the **Workspace** tab shows anything still uploading or waiting. Tap **View details** to see each item. One that failed shows **Retry now** and **Discard**. Once uploaded, captures are searchable, editable, and available to Bower in your workspace on every device.

## Capturing in your phone's browser

If you [use Bower in your phone's browser](/getting-started/install-pwa), the **Capture** tab has a capture ring with **Voice**, **Photo**, **Video**, and **Text** modes. It works the same way: capture, review, choose **Save to**, and save. Voice recordings there can be up to 60 minutes and videos up to 10 minutes.
